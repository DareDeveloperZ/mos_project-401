About MOS Project 401

Who built this project, what it covers, and how the interactive learning experience works.

MECHANICS OF SOLIDS · INTERACTIVE LEARNING PROJECT

Understand mechanics by seeing it, changing it and experimenting.

MOS Project 401 is a browser-based learning companion for Mechanics of Solids. It connects course concepts and equations with interactive models. Students can edit a stress state, inspect traction on an inclined plane, explore Mohr's circle and review ten learning modules.

Project objective

The goal is to make selected Solid Mechanics concepts easier to understand by linking mathematical expressions to visual and numerical experiments.

  • Visualize normal and shear stress on a 3D element.
  • Explore traction on planes with different orientations.
  • Connect physical plane rotation with Mohr's circle.
  • Review lecture concepts through explanations and short challenges.

What the website includes

  • 3D Stress Cube: edit six independent components of a symmetric stress matrix and inspect the arrows and readouts.
  • Traction Explorer: change the plane normal and calculate traction, normal stress and shear traction.
  • Mohr's Circle: explore the 2D stress transformation and double-angle relationship.
  • Ten learning modules: concept summaries, equations, diagrams and worked examples.
  • Practice: short numerical challenges with feedback.
